# ME Technologies Website Structure

## 1. Home Page
The home page will introduce ME Technologies and explain its main engineering services. It will include a welcome section, a short company introduction, service highlights, and a contact button.

## 2. About Us
This page will provide information about the company, its engineering focus, and its approach to technical solutions and training.

## 3. Engineering Services
This page will explain the engineering services mentioned in the company research, including electrical engineering, solar PV design, battery energy storage systems, EV charging solutions, architecture, and civil and structural engineering. The final content will be confirmed with the company.

## 4. EV Charging
This page will explain EV charging solutions, including charging equipment, solar integration, battery storage, and energy management.

## 5. Training
This page will introduce the online solar design training program and explain the use of PVsyst and SketchUp. Course fees, duration, and batch dates will be added after confirmation.

## 6. Projects
This page will display verified company projects. Any demonstration projects created during development will be clearly labelled as samples.

## 7. Blog
This page will contain useful articles about engineering, solar energy, and EV charging.

## 8. Contact Us
This page will provide the company's verified contact information and links to its official social media pages.

## 9. Get a Quote
This section will allow visitors to send an inquiry about the services they need.

## Design Considerations
The website should be responsive, easy to navigate, readable on mobile and desktop screens, and consistent with the company's verified branding.
