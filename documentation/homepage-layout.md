# ME Technologies — Homepage Layout Plan

## 1. Navigation Bar
The navigation bar will contain the company logo, Home, About Us, Services, EV Charging, Training, Projects, Blog, and Contact links. A Get a Quote button will help visitors contact the company.

## 2. Hero Section
The hero section will introduce the company with the tagline "Smart Engineering Today. Sustainable Tomorrow." It will include a short description of the company's engineering focus and a button to explore its services.

## 3. About the Company
This section will briefly explain what ME Technologies does and provide a link to the About Us page.

## 4. Engineering Services
This section will show service cards for the engineering categories identified during research. Each card will have a title, a short description, and a link to learn more.

## 5. EV Charging Solutions
This section will introduce EV charging, solar integration, battery storage, and energy management, using information approved by the company.

## 6. Training Program
This section will highlight the online solar design training program and introduce PVsyst and SketchUp.

## 7. Projects
This section will display verified company projects. Sample projects will be clearly labelled if real company examples are not available.

## 8. Contact Section
This section will provide verified contact details and a way for visitors to send inquiries.

## 9. Footer
The footer will contain the company name, important page links, and official social media links.

## Design Notes
The homepage should be clean, professional, responsive, and easy to navigate. Text should be readable, images should be relevant, and the colors should follow the company's verified brand guidelines.
