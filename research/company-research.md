## 2. Engineering Services

From the project brief and the company's available LinkedIn information, I found six main engineering service categories.

### 1. Electrical Engineering

Electrical engineering is related to the planning and design of electrical systems. It can include electrical layouts, power distribution, and checking the electrical load required for a building or project.

For the website, we can create a separate section to explain this service and show how electrical engineering supports different projects. The company's exact services should be confirmed before adding more specific claims.

### 2. Solar PV Design

Solar PV systems use sunlight to generate electricity. Their design includes planning the placement of solar panels and estimating how much electricity a system can produce.

The company's LinkedIn information mentions solar design work, including 2D and 3D layouts, drawings, 3D views, customized reports, and basic bills of materials (BOM).

We can explain these services on the website and show how solar design helps clients understand their systems before installation.

### 3. Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)

A Battery Energy Storage System stores electricity so it can be used when needed. It can also work alongside solar systems and help manage electricity demand.

The project brief mentions using BESS with EV charging systems to help reduce peak electricity demand. We can explain this purpose in simple words on the website.

### 4. EV Charging Solutions

EV charging solutions provide the equipment and electrical setup needed to charge electric vehicles.

According to the project brief, this service includes the following areas:

- Electrical load assessment and grid upgrades
- Solar PV integration
- Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)
- Energy Management System (EMS) monitoring
- AC chargers from 7–22 kW
- DC chargers up to 360 kW
- Charging standards such as CCS2, GB-T, and Type-2
- OCPP 1.6J support

This information can be used to create a dedicated EV Charging page that explains the service and its main features.

### 5. Architecture and Building Design

Architecture and building design involve planning the layout and appearance of buildings. Depending on the project, this may include floor plans, building layouts, and 3D views.

For our website, we can introduce this service and explain how planning and design help clients understand a building project before construction.

The exact design services offered by ME Technologies should be confirmed before publishing detailed claims.

### 6. Civil and Structural Engineering

Civil engineering deals with the planning and design of construction and infrastructure projects. Structural engineering focuses on making structures stable and able to support the loads placed on them.

We can give this service its own section on the website and explain its general purpose. More specific details about the company's work should be verified first.
